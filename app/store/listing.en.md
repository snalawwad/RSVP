**Description** (4000 max):

Sina keeps your event's guest list, replies and door check-in in one place, in Arabic and English.

For hosts
- See where your invites stand: who is coming, who can't make it and who hasn't replied yet.
- Send each guest their personal invite by hand on WhatsApp, and remind the ones who haven't replied.
- Add more people within your package and choose whether each guest can bring someone.
- Answer the door's questions about people who aren't on the list.
- Watch arrivals live on the night, and see who came once it's over.
- Co-hosts see the replies too.

For guests
- All the invites sent to your number in one list.
- Reply or change your reply any time, even on the day.
- Add the date to your calendar and get directions.
- Show your door pass at the door: a QR code that works offline as a screenshot.

For door staff
- Find a guest by name, phone or pass code, or scan their door pass.
- Check in part of a group, undo a mistake, and ask the hosts about someone who isn't on the list.

Sina is for events in Kuwait. Events are set up with the Sina team; the app runs the events you host and shows the invites sent to you. There is nothing to buy in the app.
